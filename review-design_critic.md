- **Major — 4-6 representation stories and scoring:** The design requires learners to provide a model or drawing, but does not say whether that work is required before submission or how it affects correctness. A learner could submit a correct equation and answer with no model, leaving the implementation unable to determine whether the question satisfies the stated contract. Define whether the model is scored, required but unscored, or optional, and specify the submission and feedback behavior.

- **Major — Session generation and twins:** Finite candidate pools and selection without replacement do not by themselves guarantee that every primary can receive a distinct twin. Earlier selections can exhaust candidates needed by a later slot, even if a valid complete assignment exists. Specify an allocation algorithm that reserves a valid twin for every primary or uses bounded backtracking, and test adversarial pool orderings as well as randomized sessions.
